# 08 — M/S (Mid-Side) Mastering (Katz)

## Math

- M (Mid) = (L + R) / 2 — center content (kick, bass, lead vocal)
- S (Side) = (L − R) / 2 — stereo content (room, panned elements)
- Reconstruct: L = M + S; R = M − S

## When to Use

When a mix problem is localized to the center or to the sides only; when broadband processing would cause collateral damage.

## M/S EQ Technique

- Bass below 250Hz → remove from S channel (convention: bass mono; side bass = muddy + phase problems)
- Air boost (10kHz+) → apply to S channel only → wider perceived presence without altering center brightness
- Buried lead vocal → boost 2–5kHz on M only
- Resonance only in center → narrow peak cut on M; leave S untouched

## M/S Compression Technique

- Compress M channel: 2:1–3:1, slow attack, moderate release → glues center; leaves stereo tail open
- Do NOT compress S heavily — kills stereo depth immediately
- Width automation trick: reduce S level in verse, restore in chorus → creates natural expansion without remixing

**Hardware:** Weiss EQ-1 (native MS mode); **Plugins:** FabFilter Pro-Q3 (per-band MS), iZotope Ozone Imager, Brainworx bx_digital V3
