## Notes
- Commission (฿) = Annual Premium × Rate (%)
- Production Count = % of FYP counted toward sales contest / agency remuneration (paid on top of commission)
- "—" = no commission for that year range
- WL17/18/19 SA tiers: 100k–199,999 OR 200,000 (exactly two tiers)
- WL20 min SA 250,000; min entry age 16
- WL21/WL23 three SA tiers: 250k–299k / 300k–499k / 500k+
- TSA/TPA/YHA/PC/PN series: commission only Yr1–5 regardless of premium term length
- WP-B/WPTR/WP-F/PB4N/PB5N/TPD3/T4MC: rates mirror the attached main policy
- WPCI: use premium term of attached main policy to select rate tier
- CI3N/CI2N SA tiers: 100k–1,499,999 OR ≥1,500,000
- MC4N age range per pay term: Pay10=20-50, Pay15=20-45, Pay20=20-40, Pay25=20-35, Pay30=20-30
- LT3N two rate tiers: Pay5-9 vs Pay10-59
- EL1N-EL4N Production: 70% for age 0-40, 100% for age 41-70
- URP3 SAM = Sum Assured Multiple (จำนวนเท่าของเบี้ยประกันภัยหลักเพื่อความคุ้มครอง)
