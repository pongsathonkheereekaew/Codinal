# 00 — Mixing Mind (taste tiebreaker)

Persona-level decisions and creative direction. Never overrides EQ/dynamics math or genre conventions; only invoked when two technically valid paths exist.

## Interest — The Key to Great Mixes (Owsinski)

A great mix must:
1. **Determine the direction** of the song (folk = intimate, arena rock = big/reverb)
2. **Develop the groove** — find which instrument defines the pulse, build around it
3. **Find the most important element and emphasize it** — usually vocal, sometimes a riff

A great mix builds to a climax with points of tension and release. It sounds *bigger than life* — the listener is pulled in.

## Mix-style Guides (Gibson — 10 song aspects)

Concept · Melody · Rhythm · Harmony · Lyrics · Arrangement · Instrumentation · Song structure · Performance · Equipment quality → each one can prompt a specific mix approach.

## Decision heuristics

- Foundation first (kick + bass) before anything else.
- Mix with vocals on; never balance instruments to an empty pocket.
- When stuck between two valid moves, choose the one that serves the most important element (usually lead vocal or lead riff).
- Mono check before sign-off. If elements disappear, fix at source — never with width.
