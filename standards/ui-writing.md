# General Writing & Typography Standards

## 1. Typography
- **Em Dash**: Always use the correct em dash symbol (`—`). Never use double hyphens (`--`) or single hyphens (`-`) for dashed pauses in text/documentation.

## 2. Professional Tone
- **Tone**: Maintain a highly objective, professional, and clear tone. Avoid exaggerated marketing fluff (e.g., "world-class", "industry-best", "game-changing") in comments, READMEs, and user-facing copy.

## 3. Plan Grilling (Concept from mattpocock/grilling)
- **Interactive sessions only**: When the user is actively present and requirements are ambiguous, ask 2-3 precise questions regarding edge cases, expected behaviors, and interface boundaries before changing existing architectures.
- **Autonomous / AFK runs** (background, cloud, remote-control sessions): do NOT block waiting for answers. Proceed with the most conservative interpretation, keep changes surgical, and list open questions in the final summary instead.
