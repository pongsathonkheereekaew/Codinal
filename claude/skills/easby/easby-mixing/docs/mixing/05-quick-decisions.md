# 05 — Quick Decisions (hot-path lookup)

Always load. First-pass answers; deeper files refine.

## Vocal Chain Order

Comp → EQ → De-esser

## Vocal Cleaning Quick Defaults (Hanes)

- RX Mouth DeClick on mouth noises
- RX DeClick (multiband) for harder clicks
- Gain -6 to -9dB on big breaths
- HPF before compressor: ~150–200Hz
- BGV: mute breaths entirely; fade in on attacks; time stacking so L/R start/end together
- Lead de-ess: CLA-76 then DeEsser @5500Hz ~-6dB; manual riding for outliers

## Mix-Stage Loudness Targets (Hanes)

- DK Audio Meter (NBC/AES full-scale): loudest part ~-6, peaks to -4
- Waves WLM ~-6 LUFS at loudest (hot mix)
- Master bus = light touches; bypass = mostly loudness diff, not tone diff
- Leave very little for mastering engineer; goal = "sounds like already mastered"

## Genre-Driven Defaults

| Genre | Vocal verb | Comp philosophy | Stereo width | Sidechain | Master comp GR |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|--------------|-----------|----------------|
| Hip-hop | Tight/dry | Heavy on 808/vox | Wide ad-libs, mono sub | 808↔kick essential | 2–3dB |
| EDM | Throws + sends | Pump aesthetic | Maximally wide | Master pump | 3–4dB |
| Metal | Slap delay > verb | Heavy parallel drums | Wide guitars hard L/R | Rare | 2–3dB |
| Jazz/Acoustic | Plate hall, sparse | Light to none | Natural | None | 0–1dB |

## Monitoring Setup (Owsinski)

- Equilateral triangle: speaker-to-speaker = listener-to-speaker
- Comfortable level — not too loud (Fletcher-Munson), not too soft (non-linearity)
- Mid-level speakers you know intimately > top-shelf unknowns
- Same speaker/room/amp for years → muscle memory > "better" gear

## Big Three (always center)

kick, bass, lead vocal.

## Mono Check Discipline

Always fold to mono before sign-off. Disappearing element = phase problem at source.
