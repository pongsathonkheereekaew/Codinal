## Gain law — MEASURED (CLEAN)
Threshold-as-drive maximizer (Waves-L family): input gained by ≈(−threshold), brickwall-limited to the ceiling.
- **Ceiling 0 dB → true-peak ≤ 0 dBFS** (4× OS peak −0.01; limited-sine RMS plateau −3.03 dB ✓). TP-clean, no overshoot.
- **Static curve** (thr −16.44, ceiling 0; out = RMS dB):
  | in dB | −36 | −33 | −30 | −27 | −24 | −21 | −18 | −15 | −12 | −9 | −6 |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | out dB | −22.2 | −18.9 | −15.6 | −12.4 | −9.1 | −5.9 | −3.6 | −3.0 | −3.0 | −3.0 | −3.0 |
  Below ~−18 dB: linear, ≈+14 dB drive. Knee ~−18..−15. Brickwall plateau (0 dBFS peak) from −15 up.
- **Latency = 51 samples** (≈1.06 ms @48k) — exact, from REAPER `TrackFX` PDC.
