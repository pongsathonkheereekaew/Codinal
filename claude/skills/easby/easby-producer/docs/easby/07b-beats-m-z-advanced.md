# 07b — Famous Drum Beats M–Z + Advanced

> Originally part of `07-famous-drum-beats.md`. Split for load efficiency. Owns alphabetical entries from "Manic Depression" through "YYZ" plus Part 2 "Fancy A Challenge" advanced beats.

**Notation key:** same as 07a — see router for glossary.

---

## Part 1 — Standard Beats (M–Z)

### Manic Depression | Jimi Hendrix | Mitch Mitchell | Blues-Rock | 3/4 (p87)
- 3/4 time. BD and ride cymbal bell on beats 1, 2, 3.
- Snare: ghost note '+' of beat 1, normal snare '+' of beat 2.
- Two high tom notes on 2nd and 3rd partial of triplet for beat 3 (LH, flows into next bar). Jazz-rooted.

### Memphis Soul Stew | King Curtis | Bernard "Pretty" Purdie | Soul/Funk (p88)
- Solid eighth notes hi-hat. Snare on beat 2 (bars 1&2), beats 2 and '+' of 4 (bars 3&4).
- All other snares = ghost notes. Ghosts: 'a' of beat 2, 'e' and 'a' of beat 3, beat 4 itself.
- BD: beat 1, '+' of 1, '+' of 3 (all 4 bars). Extra BD '+' of beat 4 in bars 1&2.

### Midlife Crisis | Faith No More | Mike 'Puffy' Bordin | Alternative Metal (p89)
- Snare 2&4. Hi-hat foot on all eighth notes. BD on 'a' of beat 2 (pushes into beat 3).
- Floor tom flam on '+' of beat 4. Sometimes adds tom accent on beat 1.

### No One Knows | Queens of the Stone Age | Dave Grohl | Stoner Rock (p90)
- Verse bar 1: BD with RH hi-hat on all four quarters. Snare 2&4.
- Bar 2: Triplet on beat 3. Snare on 3rd note '+' of triplet. Hi-hat opens on beat 4.
- At 2:59: Quarter notes crash RH, snare 2&4. BD plays quarter note triplets creating polyrhythm.

### No Woman No Cry | Bob Marley & The Wailers | Carlton Barrett | Reggae (p91)
- "One drop" beat. Cross-stick and BD played together on 2&4.
- Hi-hat on beat 1 and '+' of beat 1 (accented). Continues 'e', '+', 'a' of beat 2 with slight open on '+'.
- Half-swung/half-straight feel. Smooth and laid back.

### One | Metallica | Lars Ulrich | Heavy Metal/Thrash (p92–93)
- Climax (4:43): Unison sextuplets. Hi-hat quarter notes half-open. Strong snare 2&4.
- Bar 2: snare+crash on beat 4 and '+' of beat 4. BD: 16th note sextuplets — exactly 6 BD then snare (7th note). Requires smooth double bass pedal.
- Early section (1:15): 6/8 time. Hi-hat on all 6 beats. Snare on beat 5. Extra bar of 2/4 every 8 bars.

### Overkill | Motorhead | Phil Taylor | Thrash Metal (p94)
- Eighth notes half-open hi-hat with accents on all downbeats (1,2,3,4).
- Alternating double bass: RF on all downbeats (simultaneous with snare on 2&4); LF on all '+' upbeats.
- "Year Zero for thrash metal."

### Paint It Black | Rolling Stones | Charlie Watts | Psychedelic Rock (p95)
- RH eighth notes on high tom throughout (never leaves high tom).
- Snare on ALL FOUR beats (1,2,3,4) simultaneously with RH high tom.
- BD on beats 1&3. Tribal, energetic feel.

### Panic Attack | Dream Theater | Mike Portnoy | Progressive Metal (p96)
- Three-note repeating cell: snare+crash, then two BD notes (double bass). Cell repeated 4× as 16ths (first three beats), then twice as 16th triplets on beat 4 (acceleration effect).
- 5/8 section (0:50): Hi-hat on all 5 beats. Snare on beat 4. BD: beat 1, '+' of 2, '+' of 3. Open hi-hat beat 5.

### Paradise City | Guns N' Roses | Steven Adler | Hard Rock (p97–98)
- Intro: Snare flams 2&4. BD '+' of beat 3 into snare beat 4.
- Verse: Slightly open hi-hat (slushy), snare 2&4. BD follows guitar riff exactly.
- End (4:50) double time: 4 snares per bar. Two-note BD on downbeat of each beat. Crashes beat 1 of bars 1&2.

### Pick Up The Pieces | Average White Band | Robbie McIntosh | Funk/Jazz-Funk (p99–100)
- Open hi-hats on '+' of beats 2&3 (forward momentum). BD feathered throughout.
- Extra hi-hat 'a' of beat 2 (LH). Cross-stick on 2&4.
- Intro: Extra snare notes synced with brass on 'a' of beats 2&4.

### Pretty Woman | Roy Orbison | Buddy Harman | Rock and Roll (p101)
- Ride cymbal eighths throughout. Snare AND BD played together on every beat of both bars.
- Extra snare on '+' of beat 3 at bar 2 end (three snares in a row from beat 3).
- Middle eight: Fill from '+' of beat 3, both hands RLRL to high tom. Classic 1960s double snare drum beat.

### Pride (In The Name Of Love) | U2 | Larry Mullen Jnr. | Rock (p102–103)
- Verse: Eighths hi-hat, snare on beats 1&2 of BOTH bars (unusual!). BD: beat 1, beat 3, 'a' of 3.
- Floor tom on 'e' of beat 4 bar 2. Open hi-hat '+' of beat 4.
- Chorus: RLRL 16th hi-hat. RH comes to snare 2&4. BD: beat 1, '+' of 2, beat 3, '+' of 4.

### Quadrant 4 | Billy Cobham | Billy Cobham | Jazz-Fusion (p104)
- Traditional jazz ride cymbal pattern at very fast tempo.
- Double bass shuffle: RF on downbeats (1,2,3,4). LF on upbeat of each beat.
- Ghost note snare on middle triplet note of beats 1&3. Steady backbeat 2&4.
- **Technique:** The tempo is what makes it technically demanding.

### Raining Blood | Slayer | Dave Lombardo | Thrash Metal (p105)
- Main beat: Eighth notes hi-hat (hard downbeat accents). Alternating double bass RL, RF always falls with RH hi-hat.
- Thrash beat (vocals): Fast ride, BD on ALL downbeats (1,2,3,4), snare on ALL '+' upbeats.
- "A real right hand workout."

### Rock And Roll | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Hard Rock (p106)
- Famous intro: Starts on '+' of beat 3 (tricks listener). Both hands simultaneously on half-open hi-hat and snare.
- Quiet snares except accent notes. ~4 bars before guitar enters.
- Main beat: Same double-hand technique. Quiet snares except 2&4 accents. BD on 1, 3, '+' of 3. Bonham improvises BD throughout.

### Rock Around The Clock | Bill Haley and His Comets | Billy Gussak | Rock and Roll | 1954 (p107–108)
- Verse 1: Shuffle on snare hoop/rim RH. BD feathered all four beats. NO snare backbeat until end of bar 2: two sharp rim shots on '+' of beat 3 and beat 4.
- Verse 2: Same but single rim shot on beat 4 of bar 2.
- Verse 3: Rim shots on beat 4 and '+' of beat 4.
- Verse 4: Snare backbeat finally on 2&4 of each bar (drives to end). Drums gradually commit.

### The Rock Show | Blink-182 | Travis Barker | Pop-Punk (p109)
- Verse (0:13): Eighth notes hi-hat RH. Snare 2&4. BD: beat 1, '+' of 1, beat 3, '+' of 3.
- Open hi-hat on beat 1 of bar 1, closes with snare beat 2. Second open '+' of beat 3 bar 2.
- First opening skipped on first play-through, added on subsequent repeats.

### Rock Steady | Aretha Franklin | Bernard Purdie | Soul/Funk (p110)
- Straight eighth notes hi-hat RH. Open hi-hat '+' of beats 1&3 (leading into snare on 2&4).
- Strong snare 2&4. Ghost notes: 'a' of beat 2 (simultaneously with BD — forward momentum), 'e' of beats 2&4 (just after snare — loud to quiet transition).

### Rosanna | Toto | Jeff Porcaro | Pop-Rock (p111–112)
- **The Rosanna Shuffle (half-time shuffle):** RH plays 1st and 3rd triplet of each beat on hi-hat.
- ONE strongly accented snare per bar on beat 3 (half-time). Ghost notes on 2nd partial of EVERY triplet.
- Ghost notes right after snare accent require nimble wrist (loud→quiet instantly).
- Two ghost notes end of bar 2 on 2nd and 3rd partial of beat 4 triplet.
- **Origin:** Bonham "Fool In The Rain" shuffle feel + Purdie half-time beat.
- Pre-vocal intro: Same beat WITHOUT ghost notes.

### Roxanne | The Police | Stewart Copeland | Punk/Reggae (p113–114)
- Pre-vocal: Eighth notes hi-hat, soft open '+' of beat 4. Single snare on beat 2 only (half-time).
- BD on '+' of beat 1 and beat 2 (reggae "one drop" influence — skip beat 1).
- Vocal verse: Adds snare on beat 4.
- Pre-chorus: RH to ride cymbal bell. Bell accents on beat 3 and '+' of beat 4. LH: snare beat 2, high tom beat 4 and '+' of 4. Latin feel.

### Run To The Hills | Iron Maiden | Clive Burr | Heavy Metal (p115)
- Verse intro: Double-handed 16th notes hi-hat RLRL. BD like heartbeat on all four downbeats.
- Open hi-hat beat 4 simultaneously with BD. Both hands to toms '+' of beat 4 (RH medium, LH floor). Closes beat 1 next bar.
- Chorus (tempo doubles): Bar 1: 16th hi-hat, BD on downbeats, RH snare 2&4. Bar 2: hands to snare for guitar figure R-R-L on downbeat, '+', 'a' of each beat. Crash on beat 1 of two-bar loop.

### Scar Tissue | Red Hot Chili Peppers | Chad Smith | Alternative Rock (p116–117)
- Eighths hi-hat with accent '+' of beat 1. Snare 2&4 + additional snare 'a' of beat 2.
- BD: beat 1, 'a' of beat 1, '+' of beat 3.
- Ghost notes: 'e' of beat 1, 'e' of beats 2&4. Open hi-hat '+' of beat 4. LF bouncing hi-hat pedal continuously.

### Scentless Apprentice | Nirvana | Dave Grohl | Alternative Rock/Grunge (p118)
- Eighth notes hi-hat, snare 2&4 + flam on 'a' of beat 2.
- BD: 'e' of beats 2&4 (directly after snare). BD fills ALL of beat 3 (four notes: beat 3, 'e', '+', 'a' — requires ankle technique). Extra BD 'a' of beat 4.

### Seven Days | Sting | Vinnie Colaiuta | Pop | 5/4 (p119–120)
- 5/4 time (5 counted beats per bar). Eighth notes hi-hat with accent every 4 notes (creates 2-beat polyrhythm against 5-beat bar).
- BD on beat 1. Cross-stick on beat 4.
- Bars 7&8: hi-hat accent moves to every third note (another polyrhythm). Demanding to count.

### Sexy MF | Prince | Michael Bland | Funk/Soul (p121)
- All 16ths swung/shuffled throughout. James Brown-style with snare accents + ghost notes.
- Only two accented snares: beat 1 and '+' of beat 2 (simultaneously with BD notes).
- BD and snare ghost notes lock together for first half of bar. Snare ghosts take space between BD on 'e' and 'a' of beat 4.
- **Key:** Ghost notes as quiet as possible. Nothing competes with displaced snare backbeat on 1 and '+' of 2.

### Sing Sing Sing | Benny Goodman Orchestra | Gene Krupa | Jazz/Swing (p122)
- First two bars only. Single stroke roll between RH and LH on floor tom (RLRL).
- BD feathered lightly on all four beats. Hi-hat foot stepped on beats 2&4 (traditional jazz).
- Accented notes: what makes it come alive. Key: play non-accented notes QUIETLY, not accented notes too loudly.
- Slight swing feel. First extended drum break in recorded music.

### Smells Like Teen Spirit | Nirvana | Dave Grohl | Alternative Rock (p123)
- Chorus groove: Hi-hat half-open on downbeats 1,2,3,4. BD 'a' of beat 1, snare on beat 2. BD '+' and 'a' of beat 3, snare beat 4.
- Snare on 'a' of beat 2 flows smoothly into next four notes.
- **Famous intro fill:** Four-note pattern repeated on beats 1, 2, 3: snare flam on downbeat + hi-hat '+', then two BD on 'e' and 'a'. Beat 4 = flam + BD on '+' only.

### Soul Man | Sam and Dave | Al Jackson Jnr. | Soul (p124)
- Starts at 0:08. Eighth notes hi-hat RH. Snare backbeat 2&4 LH. Classic driving beat.
- BD on 'a' of beats 2&4 (leads into beats 3&1 — forward momentum). Classic placement used on countless recordings.
- Bridge (0:34): BD on all eighth notes (bass drum thumping with bass guitar). Unusually audible BD for funk/soul.

### Sour Times | Portishead | Geoff Barrow (programmed) / Clive Deamer (live) | Trip-Hop (p125)
- First four bars at 0:11. RH straight eighth notes hi-hat throughout.
- Snare backbeat 2&4. All other snares = ghost notes.
- BD: '+' and 'a' of beats 1&3 (just before snare on beat 2). Second BD 'a' falls between hi-hats.
- BD also on 'a' of beat 4 (flows into beat 1 of next bar).
- Ghost notes on 'a' and 'e' of beats 2&3 between hi-hats. Bar 2: ghost on 'a' of beat 2 is "buzzed" (press roll).
- Open hi-hat on '+' of beat 3 in bar 2 and bar 4.
- **Technique:** Restraint — temptation to play ghost notes loudly must be resisted.

### Stratus | Billy Cobham | Billy Cobham | Jazz-Fusion (p126–127)
- Occurs at 0:10. Recorded: eighth notes hi-hat, snare 2&4. Live: one-handed 16th notes.
- Ghost notes fill ALL gaps between hi-hats not filled by BD or accented snare.
- BD on 'e' of beat 2 (straight after accented snare note) — effective at creating "feel."
- Open hi-hat '+' of beat 4, closes beat 1 next bar.
- Variation (1:19): Snare backbeat moves from beat 2 to 'e' of beat 2. Snare beat 4 remains.
- Variation (3:18): Snare back on 2&4 but extra snare '+' of beat 4 (simultaneous with hi-hat open). Snare accent 'e' of beat 3, BD '+' of beat 3. BD 'a' of beat 2 = forward momentum.
- This section sampled by Massive Attack.

### Suck My Kiss | Red Hot Chili Peppers | Chad Smith | Funk-Rock (p128–129)
- First four bars. RH open hi-hat eighth notes (except when playing crash on beat 1 of bars 1 and 4).
- Bars 1–3 same: Snare 2&4. BD: beat 1, '+' of 1, '+' of beat 2, 'e' of beat 3.
- Bar 4 fill: BD same rhythm beats 1&3. LH two snares starting '+' of beat 4. First snare simultaneous with hi-hat on beat 4.
- Section at 0:22: BD 'e' and 'a' of beat 2 between hi-hats. Snare on its own for beat 4 bar 1 (RH stays on hi-hat throughout — LH snares). Open hi-hat sharply on 'e' of beat 4 with BD.
- Section at 1:05: 30th note drag LH on 'a' of beat 3 (very quiet). Bar 4 two extra BD starting 'e' of beat 4.

### Sunday Bloody Sunday | U2 | Larry Mullen Jr. | Rock (p130)
- Drums enter on '+' of beat 4 of a previous bar. Beat 1 = first bass drum.
- Double-handed 16th note pattern RLRL throughout on hi-hat/snare. Hands drop to snare as needed.
- BD plays steady quarter notes on ALL downbeats = military/marching sound.
- Two-note snare groupings starting on '+': RH then LH. Single snare = RH only (LH stays on hi-hat).
- Played to click track, recorded in stairwell. Military tattoo feel.

### Sunshine Of Your Love | Cream | Ginger Baker | Rock (p131)
- RH eighth notes on FLOOR TOM throughout (never touches hi-hat or ride).
- LH medium tom tom on beats 1&3 (reversed backbeat — normally snare on 2&4).
- BD: beat 2, '+' of beat 2, beat 3, '+' of beat 3. No BD on beat 1.
- **Innovation:** Reversed traditional backbeat + tom-based groove = distinctive tribal character.

### Superstition | Stevie Wonder | Stevie Wonder | Soul/Funk (p132)
- Very first four bars. BD on all four beats. Snare 2&4.
- RH plays swung 16th pattern on hi-hat (not even — felt/counted in swung/shuffled manner).
- If one-handed hi-hat too difficult, LH assists on 'e' or 'a' notes.
- **Note:** Swung/shuffled 16ths = defining quality of soul drumming.

### Take Five | Dave Brubeck | Joe Morello | Jazz | 5/4 (p133)
- 5/4 time (5 counted beats per bar). Extended version of traditional jazz ride cymbal pattern.
- Same ride cymbal pattern maintained throughout (+ improvisation). Ghost notes on 2nd and 3rd partial of triplet in beat 4.
- Live: hi-hat stepped on beats 2&4 (early) then also beat 5 (later).
- Improvises constantly — sometimes only snare on '+' of beat 2 is accented.

### There Is | Box Car Racer | Travis Barker | Pop-Punk (p134)
- Single stroke roll on snare RLRL. BD falls with RH on beats 1&3.
- Flams on 'a' of beat 1 and '+' of beat 2 (LH grace note drops in quietly first).
- Five stroke roll starting '+' of beat 3: RRLL (four 32nd notes) + accented RH on beat 4.
- Then accented LH on 'e' of beat 4.
- **Technique:** Rudiments (flams, five-stroke roll) embedded in groove context.

### Time Is Running Out | Muse | Dominic Howard | Alternative Rock (p135)
- Section at 1:46. Single stroke 16ths around toms and snare, BD bridges gaps.
- Starts LH medium tom (frees LH for high tom), RH floor tom. BD 'a' of beat 1 → both hands to snare flam beat 2.
- Pattern repeats from '+' of beat 2: RLRL around toms (medium, high, then floor toms × 3). BD 'a' of beat 3 to bridge gap.
- Both hands between floor tom and snare on '+' and 'a' of beat 3 (not a flam — simultaneous).
- Hi-hat opens '+' of beat 4, closes beat 1 next bar. Option: stamp eighth notes hi-hat foot throughout.

### Tomorrow Never Knows | The Beatles | Ringo Starr | Psychedelic Rock (p136)
- Throughout entire song. RH stays on crash cymbal creating eighth note wash.
- Snare backbeat on beat 2. Beat 4 SKIPPED — instead: two 16ths on high tom on '+' and 'a' of beat 3 (RL).
- BD on '+' of beat 2 (just after snare), then beat 3 (leading into tom part).
- **Effect:** Skipped beat 4 + tom stabs = hypnotic, non-standard groove.

### Tom Sawyer | Rush | Neil Peart | Progressive Rock (p137–138)
- First 8 bars: RH 16th notes hi-hat throughout (stops only for occasional crash). Snare 2&4.
- Neil gradually increases BD complexity: simple at start, more syncopation as progresses.
- Bars 3&4: BD on 'e' directly after snare, simultaneously as hi-hat opens. Hi-hat closes on '+' of beat 4.
- Bars 5–8: Crash on beat 2 simultaneously with snare. RH moves to crash quickly after hi-hat 'a' of beat 1.
- Section at 0:40 (ride): Super fast double BD lick on 'a' of beat 1. BD on 'a' of beat 2, 'e' of beat 3, 'a' of beat 3. Two snares: beat 4 and '+' of beat 4.
- Section at 1:35 — **7/8 time:** 7 eighth notes per bar. Snare backbeat on beat 3 and beat 7. One BD '+' of beat 4 bar 1 (open hi-hat). Second bar: BD '+' of beat 5. Two bars looped.

### Toxicity | System of a Down | John Dolmayan | Alternative Metal | 6/8 (p139–140)
- Entire song in 6/8 (6 eighth notes per bar = counted in triplet groups of 3).
- Bars 1–3: Steady eighth notes hi-hat. Fat snare on beat 4. BD: beat 1, '+' of beat 3, beat 6.
- Ghost snare '+' of beat 2 between hi-hats. Extra snare '+' of beat 5.
- Bar 4: Snare at '+' of beat 5 replaced by two 30th note ghost notes (LH) before BD on beat 6, then snare on '+' of beat 6.
- Extended version (8 bars): Bars 1&5: flams on beats 2,4,6. Bars 2&6: 32nd note flurry (RLRL) on beat 4. Bars 3&7: eighth notes half-open hi-hat, snare 2&4. Extra snares '+' of beats 2&5.

### Under The Bridge | Red Hot Chili Peppers | Chad Smith | Alternative Rock (p141)
- Section at 1:50. Four-bar pattern. Eighth notes hi-hat, cross-stick 2&4.
- BD: beat 1 and '+' of beat 2 for bars 1&3. Skip BD beat 1 of bars 2&4 — instead BD '+' of beats 2&3.
- BD on beat 3 resolves downbeat before opening hi-hat '+' of beat 4 at end of four-bar cycle.
- Break at 1:22: 16th notes hi-hat + ghosted cross-sticks. Extra cross-stick on 'a' of beat 2 and 'e' of beat 3 (ghost). Extra hi-hat 'a' of beats 1&3 (rhythmic lift).

### Walk | Pantera | Vinnie Paul | Heavy Metal/Groove Metal (p142–143)
- Main groove (0:17): Hi-hat on beats 1&3 with BD. Snare 2&4.
- BD on '+' of beat 3 = counted as triplet last note (gives swing element).
- Bar 4: Entire bar counted as triplets. BD+crash on every second triplet note starting beat 1 (quarter note triplet rhythm = 3 evenly spaced beats across the bar).
- Snare: 2&4 between BD and crash. Snare beat 2 with hi-hat; snare beat 4 with crash.
- Variation at 2:40: BD plays every other triplet note (quarter note triplet) while hi-hat holds steady quarter notes.
- Chorus (0:52): Bell of ride cymbal quarter notes RH. Crashes: beat 1 of bars 1&2, beat 3 of bar 3. BD: beat 1 and swung '+' of beat 3 (bar 1). Swung snare '+' of beat 4 before next bar.

### Walking On The Moon | The Police | Stuart Copeland | Reggae (p144–146)
- Classic reggae style. Shuffle rhythm on hi-hat (1st and 3rd triplet of each triplet).
- BD plays role of snare backbeat, falling on beats 2&4 (reversed use). Single cross-stick on beat 3.
- Stuart drops out of shuffle occasionally for quarter notes on hi-hat.
- Improvised variation (0:20): Cross-stick on '+' of beat 4 (third triplet partial — forward momentum).
- Variation (0:25): Extra BD on '+' of beat 2.
- Variation (1:27): Last triplet note of beat 4 doubled on hi-hat RL = roll into beat 1.
- Variation (2:34): At 2:34, classic reggae hi-hat lick — skip hi-hat on beat 1, play 2nd and 3rd triplet partial of beat 1 instead. Creates cool syncopated flow into BD beats 2 of both bars.
- Variation (2:45): Cross-stick plays every other triplet note = quarter note triplet polyrhythm against shuffle hi-hat. Open hi-hat on beat 4 ends it.

### Walk This Way | Aerosmith | Joey Kramer | Hard Rock/Funk-Rock (p147)
- Intro: Straight eighth notes hi-hat. Snare 2&4. BD: beat 1, beat 3, '+' of beat 3 (all with hi-hat). Extra BD 'a' of beat 2 (pushes into beat 3, between hi-hats).
- Open hi-hat beat 1 of bar (closes '+' of beat 1 simultaneously with RH hi-hat).
- Verse (0:17): Half-open hi-hat. Snare 2&4. BD: beat 1, '+' of beat 3, 'e' of beat 4 (between hi-hats, after snare). Three-note BD figure starting '+' of beat 3 ending on beat 1.

### War Pigs | Black Sabbath | Bill Ward | Heavy Metal (p148–149)
- Main groove: Hi-hat foot stepping eighth notes throughout (LF keeps time).
- Two crash cymbal stabs with guitars on beat 1 and 'e' of beat 1.
- Hi-hat struck at 'e' of beat 3 bar 2 (open due to being stepped = natural).
- Intro (6/4 time): Jazz ride cymbal pattern RH (swung). BD on beat 1. Snare on beat 4. Six counted beats per bar = very slow swung half-time.
- After guitar stabs: Crash at beginning of each bar. BD pushes: 'a' of beats 1&2 (before snare). BD two 16ths: 'a' of beat 2 and beat 3 itself. Snare 'e' of beat 3 between hi-hats.

### We Will Rock You | Queen | Roger Taylor | Rock (p150)
- Throughout entire song. Snare flams on 2&4.
- High tom + floor tom + BD together on beats 1, '+' of 1, beat 3, '+' of 3.
- Optional: flam the two toms. No hi-hat or ride — entirely toms, bass drum, snare.
- Multi-tracked huge kick drums and hand claps on original recording (impossible to fully replicate live).

### What'd I Say | Ray Charles | Milt Turner | Soul/Latin (p151)
- Two-bar pattern throughout. BD gently plays quarter notes both bars. Cross-stick backbeat on beats 2 of each bar.
- RH on ride cymbal bell: two 16ths ('+' and 'a') push into backbeats on beats 2&4 of bar 1 and beat 2 of bar 2.
- LH moves to high tom on beat 4 and '+' of beat 4 during bar 1. Bar 2: LH high tom from '+' of beat 3 (three high tom notes, RH plays bell simultaneously).
- **Style:** Latin percussionist rhythms applied to drum kit.

### When The Levee Breaks | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Hard Rock/Blues (p152)
- Throughout song. RH steady eighth notes hi-hat. Snare 2&4.
- Originally one BD on beat 1 (echo/reverb made it sound like two). Notation uses two: beat 1 and 'e' of beat 1.
- Other BD: '+' of beat 3 and 'a' of beat 3 (leading into snare on beat 4). 'a' of beat 3 falls between hi-hats.
- Recorded foot-of-stairwell. Sampled by 100s of DJs worldwide.

### Whole Lotta Love | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Hard Rock (p153)
- Verse at 0:50. Eighth notes hi-hat RH. Snare on beats 2 and '+' of beat 4 (displaced from beat 4 to '+').
- Occasional ghost notes LH between hi-hats (Bonham varies pattern as song progresses).
- BD: 'a' of beat 3 and 'e' of beat 4 (both fall between hi-hats, follow guitar riff).
- Slight swing element throughout — Bonham's personal feel, famously hard to emulate.

### Wipeout | Surfaris | Ron Wilson | Surf Rock (p154)
- Drum solo section (0:34). Medium tom (or snare with snares off) throughout.
- Both hands alternating 16ths RLRL. All accents played by RH only (LH stick stays low).
- Bar 1 accents: ALL downbeats (1,2,3,4). Bar 2 accents: beat 1, '+' of beat 2, '+' of beat 3.
- **Technique:** Accent control — RH hits slightly harder than LH; no need to pound.

### With Or Without You | U2 | Larry Mullen Jnr. | Rock (p155)
- Section at 1:46. Two-bar groove. RH eighth notes on FLOOR TOM throughout both bars.
- LH snare with floor tom on beats 2&4. BD drives on beats 1&3.
- Bar 2: Ghost snare on 'a' of beat 2. LH moves to medium tom on 'e' of beat 3 (falls between floor tom notes — RLR on beats 3, 'e', '+'). LH back to snare beat 4. Then LH to hi-hat '+' of beat 4 (opens, closes beat 1 next bar).

### Wonderwall | Oasis | Alan White | Britpop/Indie Rock (p156–157)
- Drum part starts at 0:48. RH eighth notes hi-hat throughout.
- Recurring ghost snare pattern: 'a' and 'e' immediately after each snare backbeat on 2&4. These fall between hi-hats.
- Sometimes doubles snare on 'a' of beat 4 (both bars 1&2). Two 16th notes on '+' and 'a' of beat 4 (bars 3&4).
- BD relatively simple: only on eighth notes, not between hi-hats. BD pushes '+' of beat 3 (bars 1&3).
- Chorus (1:08): Ghost notes vary — sometimes none, sometimes 'e' is ghosted and 'a' is accented (loud as backbeats).
- **Technique:** Left hand snare syncopation virtuosity — ghost note dynamics vary constantly.

### Word Up | Cameo | Larry Blackmon | Funk (p158)
- Verses: RH quarter notes on beats 1,2,3,4 + extra hi-hat '+' of beat 3. Snare 2&4. BD: beat 1 and '+' of beat 2.
- Variation (2:17): Quarter notes hi-hat only (1,2,3,4 — more space). Snare 2&4 same. Extra BD on 'a' of beat 4.
- Computer-driven, watertight backbeat character.

### You Really Got Me | The Kinks | Bobby Graham/Clem Cattini | Rock (p159)
- Steady eighth notes half-open hi-hat. Snare 2&4.
- BD follows guitar/bass riff exactly: beat 1, '+' of beat 1, '+' of beat 2.
- Break at 0:39 (two-bar break): BD: beat 1, '+' of beat 2, beat 3 (both bars). Flam on beat 2 of bar 1.
- Break exits with three snares in a row from '+' of beat 3.

### You Shook Me All Night Long | AC/DC | Phil Rudd | Hard Rock (p160)
- Verse: Eighth notes hi-hat, snare 2&4. BD: beat 1 and '+' of beat 3 (forward momentum).
- Chorus: Same hands. BD changes: '+' of beat 3 moves to beat 3 itself. BD '+' of beat 4 → pushes into beat 1 next bar.
- Occasional crash+snare on beat 2 during chorus to emphasise guitar parts.

### YYZ | Rush | Neil Peart | Progressive Rock (p161–162)
- Section at 1:14. RH stays on ride cymbal bell throughout.
- Snare 2&4. BD: beat 1, '+' of beat 2, '+' of beat 3.
- Two fast 16th notes before snare on beat 2: '+' and 'a' of beat 1 (pattern RRL with hands). Same before beat 4 except ride bell also plays on '+' of beat 3. Two 16ths after snare on beat 4: 'e' and '+' of beat 4 (pattern RRLRR).
- Intro — **10/8 time:** Whole pattern takes 10 counted beats before repeating. Stabs follow bass/guitar exactly. One stab beat 1, then 3 stabs, then 4 stabs (1, 3, 4). BD as pickup notes between stabs. Resolves with two BD notes starting beat 10.
- Bell pattern at beginning = Morse code for "YYZ."

## Part 2 — "Fancy A Challenge" Advanced Beats (p163–168)

### 6:00 | Dream Theater | Mike Portnoy | Progressive Metal (p164)
- First four bars at 0:02. Both hands on hi-hat (double-handed 16th notes RLRL). Hands drop to drums as needed.
- Bars 1&3: RLRL hi-hat. RH to snare on beat 2. LH to 'a' and 'e' of beats 3&4.
- BD on 'a' of beat 2 (with LH, simultaneously with hi-hat open for one 16th).
- Bar 3: Crash beat 1, open hi-hat '+' of beat 1. At beat 2: snare, high tom, medium tom, crash (RLRL).
- Bar 4: Thirty-second note line RLRL on hi-hat. Groups of six: snare drum, BD, snare drum, BD (6 notes apart each) creating cross-beat polyrhythm.

### Constant Motion | Dream Theater | Mike Portnoy | Progressive Metal (p165–166)
- First 9 bars at 0:08. Odd time signatures: 5/8 and 6/8 alternating. Also bar of 15/16.
- Eighth note rate constant throughout all time signature changes.
- Bars 1,2,3,4: Eighth notes half-open hi-hat + snare on third eighth note of each bar. Crash cymbal LH after fourth eighth note in bars 2&4. Bar 4 has extra crash on beat 4 before open hi-hat 'a' of beat 4.
- Bars 5&6: Same as 1&2 but double bass drum line fills first eighth note (4 thirty-second notes) + two more 16th BD notes.
- Bar 7: Double bass start + four notes then snare beat 3, then crash/splash/ride bell notes in row.
- Bar 8: Same beginning then 16ths around drums from beat 3, crash on 'a' of beat 4.
- **Technique:** Learn guitar riff by heart — locks drum part naturally.

### Oakland Stroke | David Garibaldi | David Garibaldi | Funk (p167)
- Throughout track. Hi-hat on downbeat and 'a' of each beat (dotted eighth note shuffle pattern).
- Opens hi-hat on beat 4 of bar 1 and beat 1 of bar 2.
- All snare notes ghosted UNLESS marked with accent. Ghost/accent switching constantly.
- Sometimes double note with accent on second note ('e' and '+' of beat 1). Sometimes double with accent on first note ('+' and 'a' of beat 3 bar 2). Sometimes accent alone ('e' of beat 2 bar 1).
- **Technique:** Work hands first without BD. Then add BD later. Very slowly, one line at a time.
- Garibaldi freely changes ghost notes, accents throughout.

### Stream Of Consciousness | Dream Theater | Mike Portney | Progressive Metal | 5/4 (p168)
- Section at 0:40. 5/4 time (5 counted beats per bar).
- All four bars same: Snare LH on '+' of beat 2 and beat 5. LH to 'a' of beat 1 and 'e' of beat 4 on hi-hat.
- BD: beat 1, beat 2, '+' of beat 3, '+' of beat 4.
- Bars 1&2: Crash/splash on beat 1. Hi-hat opens beat 3, closes '+' of beat 4 simultaneously with BD.
- Bar 2: Ride cymbal bell on beat 3. Floor tom '+' of beat 5. Bar 4: Floor tom on beat 3 with splash. Another splash beat 3. Ride bell '+' of beat 5.
