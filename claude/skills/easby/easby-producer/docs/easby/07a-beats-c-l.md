# 07a — Famous Drum Beats A–L

> Originally part of `07-famous-drum-beats.md`. Split for load efficiency. Owns alphabetical entries from "Could You Be Loved" through "Lust For Life".

**Notation key:** 16th grid positions = 1, e, +, a (per beat). Triplet = 1, trip, let. Ghost notes = quiet, marked in (brackets). Flam = two near-simultaneous notes, grace note first. Accent = >, open hi-hat = °, sticking R/L.

---

## Part 1 — Standard Beats (A–L)

### Could You Be Loved | Bob Marley & The Wailers | Carlton Barrett | Reggae (p41)
- One-hand hi-hat on '+', 'a', downbeat of each beat. Accent hi-hat on '+' of each beat.
- Bass drum four-on-the-floor. Snare on beats 2&4 simultaneous with bass drum.
- **Technique:** "One drop" variant — hi-hat accent drives forward momentum without removing beat 1.

### Da Doo Ron Ron | The Crystals | Hal Blaine | Early Rock/Pop | 12/8 (p42)
- 12/8 time. Double-handed hi-hat RLRL. Left drops to snare on beats 2&4. Bass drum on all four beats.
- Key: align hi-hat, snare, bass drum precisely on beats 2&4.

### Dance Of Eternity | Dream Theater | Mike Portnoy | Progressive Metal (p43–44)
- Mixed meter: bars 1&3 = 4/4, bar 2 = 7/8, bar 4 = 5/8. Eighth-note rate constant.
- Bar 2: linear drumming — splash cymbal → bass drum → snare → hi-hat × 2 → bass drum+crash.
- **Technique:** Linear drumming (no simultaneous notes). Count in eighths, only the grouping changes.

### Dance With The Devil | Cozy Powell | Cozy Powell | Rock/Dance (p44–45)
- Single stroke roll around toms, both hands. LH stays high tom, RH moves. Bass drum quarters.
- Accents on 'a' of beat 2, 'e' and 'a' of beat 3 (three consecutive off-beat LH accents).
- Recorded with layered overdubbed tracks.

### Dani California | Red Hot Chili Peppers | Chad Smith | Funk-Rock (p46)
- RH eighth notes hi-hat + LF bouncing pedal simultaneously (creates "swish" sound).
- Snare 2&4. Bass drum: beat 1, '+' of 1, beat 3, '+' of 3, 'a' of beat 2.
- 32nd note drag (ghost notes) LH at 'a' of beat 4.

### Don't Stand So Close To Me | The Police | Stewart Copeland | Punk/Reggae (p47–48)
- One-hand 16th notes RH hi-hat. Cross-stick on ALL downbeats (1,2,3,4). Bass drum on 2&4 with cross-stick.
- Accented hi-hat '+' beats 1&3; open hi-hat '+' beats 2&4, closes on next downbeat.
- Chorus: bell of ride on 1,2,3,'+' of 3, '+' of 4. Snare on 2&4.

### Dr Feelgood | Motley Crue | Tommy Lee | Hard Rock/Metal (p49)
- Straight eighths hi-hat. Snare 2&4. Bass drum skips beat 1, plays 'e' and '+' of beat 1.
- Open hi-hat stab with first BD, closes on beat 2 snare. Extra BD on 'a' of beat 4.
- Chorus: ride bell accents on all downbeats. BD: '+' of 1, 'e' of 2, beat 3, 'a' of 4.

### Englishman In New York | Sting | Manu Katche | Pop/Jazz/Reggae (p50)
- Cross-stick on '+' of beats 2&4 (upbeat placement = more lift). Hi-hat on '+' of each beat.
- Bar 2 fill: hi-hat doubles on '+' of beats 3&4 with simultaneous cross-sticks.
- Break at 2:30: flams on bass drum and snare on 2&4.

### Everlong | Foo Fighters | Dave Grohl/Taylor Hawkins | Alternative Rock (p51)
- 7-bar phrase. Alternating 16th hi-hat RLRL throughout all 7 bars. Snare 2&4 each bar.
- Bars 1–6 BD: '+' of 1, beat 3, '+' of 3. Bar 7 BD: beat 1, '+' of 1, beat 3, '+' of 3.
- Mini snare fill at bar 7 end: '+' and 'a' of beat 4. Relaxed, loose hands required.

### Everybody Wants To Rule The World | Tears For Fears | Chris Hughes (programmed) | Synth-Pop (p52)
- Quarter note triplet hi-hat: eighth on hi-hat then eighth rest = hi-hat on every 2nd eighth triplet.
- Snare 2&4. Bass drum on 1&3. Electronic shakers layered (impossible live solo).
- Hi-hat plays just after each bass drum and simultaneously with each snare.

### Fell In Love With A Girl | The White Stripes | Meg White | Garage Rock (p53)
- Crash + bass drum on beat 1. Two snares on beat 2 AND '+' of beat 2 with crash.
- Single crash+snare on beat 4. Hi-hat on beat 3. Extremely minimal.

### Fields Of Gold | Sting | Vinnie Colaiuta | Pop (p54)
- Single consistent beat throughout. Crisp even eighth notes hi-hat RH.
- Cross-stick on 2&4 simultaneously with hi-hat. Bass drum only on beat 1 and '+' of beat 2.
- **Lesson:** Extreme space and sparseness = grace.

### Fire | Jimi Hendrix Experience | Mitch Mitchell | Blues-Rock/Psychedelic (p55)
- 11 bars of improvised playing. Quarter notes hi-hat, varying kick/snare.
- Ghost notes in bars 3, 4, 10 with LH between hi-hats.
- 16th note triplets RLRL from '+' of beat 4 at bar 10 end.

### Fireball | Deep Purple | Ian Paice | Hard Rock (p56)
- Alternating double bass drum on ALL 16th positions (both feet, all four beats).
- Ride cymbal eighth notes RH. Snare 2&4. Three-note snare pattern cycling through positions.
- **Technique:** Double bass sixteenth notes + melodic ride. Ian improvises heavily.

### Fool In The Rain | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Rock/Shuffle (p57)
- Shuffle hi-hat: 1st and 3rd triplet of each beat. Half-time feel: ONE strong snare on beat 3.
- Ghost notes on 'trip' of beats 2&4 (between shuffle hi-hats).
- BD: beat 1, 'let' of 1, 'let' of 2, 'let' of 4. Open hi-hat on 'let' of beat 1 closes on beat 2.
- All bass drums fall simultaneously with hi-hat.

### Fools Gold | The Stone Roses | Alan 'Reni' Wren | Indie/Dance (p58)
- Unusual 2.5-bar (10-beat) loop = two 4/4 bars + one 2/4 bar.
- RLRL alternating hi-hat: LH plays 'e' and 'a'; RH plays backbeats 2&4; all BD falls with RH.
- **Effect:** 10-beat loop goes organically out of phase with song.

### Funky Drummer | James Brown | Clyde Stubblefield | Funk | 1969 (p59)
- 16th notes hi-hat RH. Open hi-hat on 'e' of beats 2&4.
- Accented snare 2&4. Ghost notes: hardest = 'a' of beat 3 (immediately before snare on 4 — requires nimble wrist).
- Most sampled drum beat in history.

### Get Up (Sex Machine) | James Brown | John 'Jabo' Starks | Funk (p60)
- All 16ths slightly swung. Open hi-hat on '+' of beats 1&3.
- Ghost notes: '+' of beat 2 (simultaneous with hi-hat — RH demanding), 'a' of beats 2, 3, 4.
- BD on '+' of beat 3 simultaneously with hi-hat opening.

### Give It Away | Red Hot Chili Peppers | Chad Smith | Funk-Rock (p61)
- Heavy open hi-hats throughout. Strong snare 2&4.
- BD syncopated: 'e' of beat 3, '+' of beats 3&4.

### Go | Pearl Jam | Dave Abbruzzese | Alternative Rock/Grunge (p62)
- Steady eighth notes hi-hat, snare 2&4.
- BD: 'a' of beat 1, '+' of beat 2, 'e' of beat 3, 'a' of beat 3.
- BD pushes into snare on beats 2&4.

### God Save The Queen | Sex Pistols | Paul Cook | Punk (p63)
- Quarter notes hi-hat, snare 2&4.
- Bar 1 BD: follows guitars on all eighths between snare backbeats. Extra quiet snare 'a' of beat 4.
- Bar 2 BD: '+' of beats 2&3 only.

### Good Times Bad Times | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Hard Rock (p64)
- Cowbell (or ride bell) RH throughout. Snare on 2, 'e' of 2, 4, 'e' of 4.
- Bar 1 BD: three in a row starting 'e' of beat 3 (single-footed triplet).
- **Bonham BD Triplets (at 0:24):** BD on 2nd and 3rd partial of each 16th note triplet group while cowbell holds steady quarter notes. Master class in bass drum technique.

### Green Onions | Booker T and the MG's | Al Jackson Jnr. | Soul/R&B (p65)
- Quarter notes ride cymbal RH. Bass drum "feathered" on all four beats (heel-down, barely heard).
- Snare 2&4 LH simultaneously as hi-hat foot steps.
- **Lesson:** Hypnotic groove through absolute unwavering consistency.

### Highway Star | Deep Purple | Ian Paice | Hard Rock (p66)
- Verse (at 0:38): Half-open hi-hat quarters RH, snare 2&4.
- Bar 1 BD: beat 3 and '+' of beat 3. Bar 2 BD: '+' of beats 2&3.
- Bar 4: crash cymbals follow guitar/organ melody — crashes interrupt quarter note hi-hat.
- Intro (0:16): Eighth notes snare only building; then accents on 2&4 added.

### Highway To Hell | AC/DC | Phil Rudd | Hard Rock (p67)
- Verse: Constant eighths hi-hat, snare 2&4, BD on 1&3 + extra BD '+' of beat 4.
- Chorus (8 bars): Half-open hi-hat, crashes on beat 1 of bars 1,3,5,7 and beats 3&4 of bars 2,4,6.
- BD every quarter note through bar 7. Two extra snares end of bar 7 (beat 4 and '+' of 4).

### Honky Tonk Woman | Rolling Stones | Charlie Watts | Rock (p68)
- Steady eighths hi-hat. Snare 2&4. BD on '+' of beats 3&4.
- Cowbell overdubbed introduction separate from drum track.

### Hot For Teacher | Van Halen | Alex Van Halen | Hard Rock/Heavy Metal (p69)
- Double bass drum shuffle (at 0:53): Main BD on all four downbeats, secondary BD on '+' upbeats.
- Ride cymbal bell on 1st and 2nd triplet partials of beats 1&3; 1st triplet partial of beats 2&4.
- Snare 2&4. Triplet ride cymbal stretches/moves closer to snare than written.

### Hot Fun In The Summertime | Sly & The Family Stone | Gregg Errico | Funk | 12/8 (p70)
- 12/8 counted 1–12. Constant eighth notes hi-hat.
- Snare on beats 4 and 10. BD on 1, 6, 7, 12. Bar 2 adds shuffled BD before snares on beats 3&9.

### I Keep Forgettin' | Michael McDonald | Jeff Porcaro | Soft Rock/Funk (p71)
- One-hand 16th notes hi-hat. Cross-stick then snare on 2&4.
- Hi-hat opens '+' of beat 4, closes beat 1 next bar.
- BD bars 1&2: beat 1, 'a' of beat 2, beat 3. Variation: '+' of 1, 'e' of 2, 'a' of 2, beat 3.

### Immigrant Song | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Hard Rock (p72)
- Eighth notes hi-hat. Strong snare 2&4 + ghost note 'a' of 2&4 (between hi-hats).
- BD: downbeats 1&3, then two 16th BD notes before each snare ('+' and 'a' of 1&3).
- Final BD on 'e' directly after each snare. Very powerful.

### In Bloom | Nirvana | Dave Grohl | Alternative Rock/Grunge (p73)
- Intro: Crashes beat 1 and '+' of beat 2 matching guitar riff. Half-open hi-hat in between.
- Two snare flams 'e' and '+' of beat 3. BD on 'a'.
- Verse (0:13): 16th notes half-open hi-hat, snare 2&4, BD on beat 1 AND 'e' of 1, beat 3 AND 'e' of 3.

### In The Air Tonight | Phil Collins | Phil Collins | Pop/New Wave (p74)
- Famous fill (at 3:17): Three-note cell (two 16ths + BD) repeated descending through toms × 4. Ends with two eighth notes on lowest tom on beat 4 and '+'.
- Post-fill groove (4 bars): Snare flams 2&4. BD and snare follow melody. Fills improvised every 4 bars.

### Jailhouse Rock | Elvis Presley | DJ Fontana | Rock and Roll | 1957 (p75)
- Quasi-shuffle hi-hat (feel shifts between swung and straight throughout).
- BD on all four beats. Snare 2&4. Historic 1957 recording.

### Kashmir | Led Zeppelin | John Bonham | Hard Rock (p76)
- Eighth notes hi-hat, snare 2&4 (simultaneous with hi-hat).
- Double BD: beat 1 AND 'e' of beat 1 (2nd falls between hi-hats). Beat 3 AND 'e' of beat 3.
- "Simple. Powerful. Great."

### Killing In The Name | RATM | Brad Wilk | Alternative Metal/Rap-Rock (p77–78)
- Main beat: Half-open hi-hat. Crash beat 1 of bar 1. BD and snare on 1&3, 2&4.
- Bar 4 fill: BD on 'e' and 'a' of beat 4.
- Intro (0:08): Closed eighths hi-hat, single open '+' of beat 4. BD 1&3. Snare 2&4 + extra 'e' of beat 3.
- Bridge (2:01): Eighths half-open hi-hat, all 16ths slightly swung. BD on 1 and 'e' of 1 (swung toward '+').

### Lapdance | NERD | Eric Fawcett | Hip-Hop/Rock (p79)
- Double-handed 16th notes hi-hat RLRL. Snare 2&4 with RH.
- Bar 1 BD: beat 1, '+' of 1, '+' of 3. Bar 2 adds BD 'e' of beat 4 (with LH).
- Hi-hat accents: 'a' of beat 2 (LH louder) and 'e' of beat 3 (LH louder) — offbeat hand accents.

### Like Eating Glass | Bloc Party | Matt Tong | Post-Punk/Indie Rock (p80–81)
- Fast eighth notes hi-hat + crash on beat 1. Three BD notes: beat 1, '+' of 1, beat 2.
- Displaced snare on '+' of beat 2 (not beat 2 itself). Extra snare 'e' of beat 3, beat 4.
- BD on '+' of beats 3&4. Bar 4 ends with snare fill (beat 4 + 'e' of 4), open hi-hat '+' of 4.

### Like I Love You | Justin Timberlake | Pharrell Williams | R&B/Pop (p82)
- Eighth notes hi-hat with loud accents on downbeats 1,2,3,4. Ghost snares between hi-hats.
- Solid snare backbeat 2&4 (all other snares are ghost notes). BD on 'a' of beat 1, 'e' of beat 4.
- Variation (0:31): snare on 'a' of beat 2 played louder; 'e' of beat 3 left out (breathing space).

### Longview | Green Day | Tre Cool | Punk Rock (p83–84)
- Verse: Shuffle between both hands. RH on floor tom throughout. LH high tom as backbeat.
- BD with RH on floor tom beat 1. LH high tom beat 2. Beat 3: RH floor tom + LH middle triplet note + BD simultaneously. Beat 4: LH high tom, RH floor tom last triplet '+'.
- Chorus: Driving swung shuffle. Quarter notes half-open hi-hat, snare 2&4. BD on 1st and 3rd triplet note of each beat (not with snares).

### Love Rears Its Ugly Head | Living Colour | Will Calhoun | Funk-Metal (p85)
- All 16ths swung throughout ('e' and 'a' played slightly later).
- Eighth notes hi-hat. Open hi-hat beat 1 and '+' of beat 1.
- BD bars 1,3,5,7: beat 1, '+' of beat 2, beat 3. BD bars 2,4,6: beat 1, 'e' of 1, 'a' of 1.
- **Technique:** Heel/toe for quiet BD notes.

### Lust For Life | Iggy Pop | Hunt Sales | Punk/Rock (p86)
- Quarter notes half-open hi-hat. Snare 2&4. BD progressive: starts bar 1 downbeat, moves to upbeat of each successive beat through bar 2.
- Double-tracked drums (two separate drum tracks). Floor tom rhythm on second track.
